## Reciprocity Map & Gift Object

Let’s start with the main questions.

**Why did I change my thesis topic? WHY?!**

I will address this in more depth in later blog posts.

For the short explanation: after going through resources, going in circles, and experiencing moments of complete mental exhaustion, I realized that my previous topic, educational support tools for K-8 teachers, was not truly my choice. It was a soulless reach born out of a desperate search for a topic and job stability. It felt like the right thing to choose for the wrong reasons.

Now, I have poured more heart and soul into my genuine areas of interest, choosing a topic deeply connected to me, my parents, and my home. We live with technology. Technology is part of us in many shapes and forms, and I want to make sure that smart home technology serves as a non-intrusive support system for my parents during their retirement. This area of lived curiosity has opened many doors, each revealing multiple questions.However, my two core questions are:

### Core Question 1

*Can smart homes really be helpful, or are they just a burden to learn?*

**What do all these questions and maps mean?**

It means I want to see my parents live their best lives with the confidence that their hard work has paid off. I want to explore the smart home technology space because, now more than ever, smart homes have become accessible to everyone. It is no longer a luxury; it is a tool with huge potential for personalized accessibility.
